Week 2: Topic Lock & Requirements

1. 项目初衷与动机 (Project Motivation)

While running is highly beneficial, it often suffers from a severe lack of immediate emotional reward. Traditional running applications focus exclusively on quantitative metrics (pace, distance, calories). For beginners and casual runners, constantly checking the screen to monitor progress is not only dangerous (introducing a safety hazard) but also critically disrupts their "Flow State".

Design Rationale & Problem Gap: Existing solutions either provide cold, robotic audio updates ("You have run 1 kilometer") or require users to look at their screens to see their virtual progress. There is a distinct lack of hands-free, emotionally intelligent accompaniment.

Our Solution: EchoRun. We introduce an "Eyes-free Event-Triggered AI Ghost Pacer". Runners can choose a distinct AI persona (e.g., a "Toxic Coach" or a "Supportive Friend"). The app monitors the runner's pace locally and triggers highly contextual, witty audio feedback at critical moments (e.g., falling behind, sprinting), turning a solitary workout into an engaging, auditory multiplayer race.

2. 用户画像 (Personas - 10/80/10 AI Principle)

To ensure our audio-feedback design caters to different emotional needs, we adapted our user personas using the 10-80-10 AI workflow to fit the new "EchoRun" concept.

Persona 1: The Reluctant Beginner

Name: Alex Chen (20, Design Student at XJTLU)

"Jogging alone feels like a chore, and looking at my slow pace on the screen just makes me anxious."

Frustrations: Intimidated by visual metrics; gets bored easily; mechanical audio-guides feel like homework.

How EchoRun Helps: Alex can select the "Gentle Supporter" AI persona. Instead of bombarding him with numbers, the AI provides timely, emotionally uplifting verbal encouragement through his earbuds, helping him stay relaxed and focused on his breathing.

Persona 2: The Tactical Socializer

Name: Brian Wang (22, CS Student at XJTLU)

"I need someone to push me. If there's no competition, I lose my motivation to sprint."

Frustrations: Misses the adrenaline of team sports; hates pulling out his phone while sprinting to check his ghost data.

How EchoRun Helps: Brian selects the "Sarcastic Coach" persona and races against his friend's previous record. When he falls behind by 10 meters, the AI instantly triggers a snarky audio taunt, fueling his competitive spirit without him needing to glance at the screen.

🤖 AI 使用记录与反思 (Technical Reflection)

Task: Pivoting project direction, refining technical architecture, and adapting Personas.

Process & Prompting: We utilized conversational AI to pivot from our previous LBS turf-war idea to a more running-centric solution. We discussed the cognitive load of interacting with screens while running and the high cost/latency of using real-time AI Agents.

Human Refinement & Decision: We rejected the complex Agent architecture in favor of a "Glanceable UI + Event-Triggered Local Logic". We decided to adopt the "Wizard of Oz" evaluation method for our prototype, pre-rendering AI persona voices (TTS) to perfectly simulate the emotional impact of the audio without the API latency, allowing us to focus strictly on testing the HCI experience.
