Week 2: Topic Lock & Requirements

1. 项目初衷与动机 (Project Motivation)

Despite the physical benefits of running, maintaining a consistent routine is notoriously difficult, especially for beginners. Traditional running applications focus heavily on quantitative metrics (pace, distance, calories), which often exacerbate the monotony of the exercise.

Design Rationale & Stakeholder Feedback: During our initial research, a running club organizer suggested a "Cultural Check-in Run" around Dushu Lake. However, from an HCI perspective, we rejected this idea because forcing runners to stop and listen to historical stories critically disrupts their "Flow State". Furthermore, static check-in points suffer from a severe retention crisis (zero replayability).

Our Solution: Geo-PolyRun. We are shifting the paradigm from "running for metrics" to "running for strategy and team synergy." By utilizing a "Hold-to-Capture Nodes" and "Post-run Polygon Enclosure" mechanism, we turn a complex, grid-like physical environment (e.g., XJTLU South Campus) into a dynamic, multiplayer territory-capture game.

2. 用户画像 (Personas - 10/80/10 AI Principle)

To better understand our target audience and guide our design decisions, we generated two distinct Personas using the 10-80-10 AI workflow (10% human context prompting, 80% AI generation, 10% human refinement based on XJTLU context).

Persona 1: The Reluctant Beginner

Name: Alex Chen (20, Design Student at XJTLU)

"I know I need cardio, but jogging around the track alone feels like a punishment."

Frustrations: Intimidated by pace metrics; gets bored easily; traditional audio-guides feel like homework.

How Geo-PolyRun Helps: The "Golden Node" blind box mechanism gives Alex short bursts of dopamine, turning a monotonous run into a treasure hunt. The focus is on capturing points for the team, making him forget about his slow pace.

Persona 2: The Tactical Socializer

Name: Brian Wang (22, CS Student at XJTLU)

"I don't mind breaking a sweat, as long as there's a strategy involved and I can win with my team."

Frustrations: Standard fitness apps lack real-time competition; GPS tracking apps often glitch and ruin the game.

How Geo-PolyRun Helps: The "Strategy Phase" (post-run polygon drawing) perfectly matches his gamer mindset. He can plan "stacking" tactics, and the Tangible AR reward satisfies his need for a shareable victory moment.

🤖 AI 使用记录与反思 (Technical Reflection)

Task: Brainstorming core mechanics, solving technical bottlenecks, and drafting Personas.

Process & Prompting: We used conversational AI to critically evaluate our initial LBS game ideas. We prompted the AI to identify potential exploits (e.g., players capturing 4 corners of a map to win instantly) and technical flaws (e.g., polygon self-intersection errors).

Human Refinement: The AI suggested using Turf.js for area calculation and proposed an elegant "Action-Strategy Loop" (running first, drawing polygons later) to prevent errors and enhance post-run socialization. We refined the AI-generated Personas to specifically fit the demographic of XJTLU students.
